About Me

Hello, and welcome!

I’m Mathias, a student at Østfold University College (HiØ) with a passion for cybersecurity and gaming. I’ve had the chance to work in both areas, from leading the Imperial Black Legion mod development team to performing cybersecurity analysis for Tilt.Work. Along the way, I’ve also taken on various freelance web development projects.

Today, I’m focused on security reviews, practical hardening, and helping people make better decisions about risk.

What I Do

Security

I work with security as a practical discipline: understanding risk, identifying weaknesses, and improving how systems are configured and used. I care about making security actionable; clear findings, sensible prioritization, and documentation that stakeholders can actually use.

Web Development

Web development was my entry point into computer science. I’ve built maintainable, user-friendly websites through both studies and freelance work; including a major project for the Imperial Black Legion. These days I approach web with a security mindset: clarity, reliability, and fewer sharp edges.

Admin

For the past eight years, I’ve worked as a server/system administrator for the Imperial Black Legion gaming community. I’m used to managing hosted services, coordinating with providers, and troubleshooting mixed stacks across platforms. My focus is stable systems, sensible access control, and fast, calm problem solving.

Management

I’ve managed teams inside a community of roughly 750 members, including Development and Community Operations. That means setting direction, coordinating people, keeping quality consistent, and delivering over time — including building a development team from scratch and running long-term projects end-to-end.
